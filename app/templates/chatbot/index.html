How it Works

The ResHub Literature Review Assistant helps you research academic topics by:

  1. Searching open-access repositories like arXiv, Semantic Scholar, and CORE
  2. Analyzing hundreds of relevant research papers
  3. Generating concise, properly cited answers to your questions

Tips for good questions:

  • Be specific about your research topic
  • Include key terms relevant to your field
  • Specify time periods if recent research is needed
